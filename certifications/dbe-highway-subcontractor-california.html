DBE Certification for Highway Subcontractors in California

Highway and roadwork specialties—striping, traffic control, guardrail, paving, and signage—are the heart of California's DBE demand. Caltrans and FHWA-funded projects carry participation goals on nearly every contract, and certified highway subcontractors are exactly who primes need.

Crown Consulting helps California highway subcontractors get DBE certified and positioned for the work primes most often set aside to meet their goals.

Why Highway Work Drives DBE Demand

Caltrans is the lead DBE certifier in California and administers most highway work in the state, and Federal Highway Administration funding attaches DBE participation goals to most projects. Well-defined highway subcontract scopes—striping, traffic control, guardrail, paving—are routinely used by primes to hit those goals, which means steady demand for certified firms.

Common Highway Subcontract Scopes and NAICS

Highway and road construction is classified under NAICS 237310 (Highway, Street, and Bridge Construction), frequently combined with specialty-trade codes for striping, traffic control, or guardrail work. Carrying the codes that match your actual specialties is what makes you findable in the CUCP directory for the right scopes.

Commercially Useful Function on Highway Jobs

As on any federally funded job, DBE credit requires a commercially useful function under 49 CFR 26.55—you must self-perform and manage your scope with your own crews and equipment rather than acting as a pass-through. Crown helps you structure and document operations so your role holds up to a commercially useful function review.

Eligibility Essentials

The certification framework is the same as any California DBE: at least 51% ownership and real day-to-day control by one or more owners who can demonstrate individual social and economic disadvantage, a personal net worth under $2.047 million, and a Personal Narrative with supporting evidence. For the full framework, see our main DBE certification page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which agency certifies DBE highway subcontractors in California?

Certification runs through the California Unified Certification Program (CUCP), with Caltrans as the lead certifier for most highway and transportation applicants. Certification by any CUCP agency is recognized by every USDOT-funded agency statewide.

What NAICS code fits a DBE highway subcontractor?

Highway and road work is classified under NAICS 237310 (Highway, Street, and Bridge Construction), often combined with specialty-trade codes for striping, traffic control, or guardrail. We align your codes to your scopes.

Why is highway work the biggest source of DBE opportunities?

Caltrans and FHWA-funded projects carry DBE participation goals on most contracts, and highway subcontract scopes are well-defined and frequently used by primes to meet those goals.
