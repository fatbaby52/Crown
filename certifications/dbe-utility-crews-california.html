DBE Certification for Utility Crews & Water Contractors in California

Utility crews—line clearance, utility relocation, and water and wastewater work—show up on federally funded transportation and infrastructure projects constantly, often as relocation scopes inside larger jobs. For utility and water/sewer contractors, DBE certification turns that demand into contracts.

Crown Consulting helps California utility and water contractors get DBE certified—and we understand this work from the inside.

Where Utility Crews Fit Federally Funded Work

Utility relocation, including water and sewer line work, is a common scope inside federally funded transportation projects, and there are standalone water and wastewater projects funded through federal programs as well. Power and communication line work and line clearance also appear on these jobs—all scopes where a certified DBE crew can earn participation credit for the prime.

NAICS for Utility and Water Contractors

Water and sewer work is classified under NAICS 237110 (Water and Sewer Line and Related Structures Construction), and power and communication line work under NAICS 237130 (Power and Communication Line and Related Structures Construction). We align your codes to the utility scopes you actually self-perform so primes find you for the right work.

Commercially Useful Function

DBE credit requires a commercially useful function under 49 CFR 26.55—you must perform and manage your scope with your own crews and equipment, not act as a pass-through. Crown helps you structure and document operations so your role holds up to a commercially useful function review.

Eligibility Essentials

The certification framework is the same as any California DBE: at least 51% ownership and real day-to-day control by one or more owners who can demonstrate individual social and economic disadvantage, a personal net worth under $2.047 million, and a Personal Narrative with supporting evidence. For the full framework, see our main DBE certification page.
