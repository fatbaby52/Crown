Missed the April 16, 2026 DBE Reevaluation Deadline?

You can still submit. Caltrans is accepting late DBE reevaluation packets—but until yours is submitted and approved, your firm is ineligible to participate in the DBE Program. Late packets are processed after all on-time submissions, so every week you wait pushes you further back in the queue.

What Happened on April 16

April 16, 2026 was the Caltrans deadline for California-certified DBE firms to submit reevaluation packets under the October 2025 USDOT Interim Final Rule, which suspended the group-based presumption of disadvantage and required every disadvantaged owner to prove disadvantage individually. All 6,000+ firms certified through the California Unified Certification Program (CUCP) were required to refile.

Per Caltrans guidance effective March 2, 2026, firms that have not submitted the required documentation are ineligible to participate in the DBE Program until they submit and are approved under the new standards.

What Being "Ineligible" Means for Your Business

  • Your firm cannot be counted as a DBE on federally funded work until your packet is approved
  • Primes looking for certified DBE partners can't rely on your listing while you're out of the program
  • If you don't intend to seek reevaluation at all, Caltrans asks that you notify them so your firm can be removed from the DBE database

One mitigating fact: during the reevaluation period, recipients may not set DBE contract goals or count DBE participation toward goals at all. The window to restore your status before goals resume is exactly why late filers should move now rather than later.

What Your Late Packet Must Include

A late packet has the same requirements as an on-time one, submitted through the Caltrans DBE System (B2Gnow):

  • Personal Narrative from each disadvantaged owner—an individualized, evidence-backed account of social and economic disadvantage. This is the slowest piece to assemble and the most common reason firms missed the deadline in the first place. See our Personal Narrative guide.
  • Current Personal Net Worth statement on the USDOT form in the B2G system, with complete supporting documentation for every entry—full statements, not screenshots, with dates matching the "as of" date on the form
  • Updated ownership and control documentation—operating agreement or bylaws, stock records, license records, signature authority

Because late packets sit behind the on-time queue, an incomplete submission that draws a request for additional information can add months. Submit it right the first time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I still submit my DBE reevaluation packet after April 16, 2026?

Yes. Caltrans has confirmed that firms that missed the April 16, 2026 deadline can still submit a DBE reevaluation packet through the Caltrans DBE System. Late packets are processed after all on-time submissions have been reviewed.

Is my firm still a certified DBE if I didn't submit?

Under Caltrans guidance effective March 2, 2026, firms that have not submitted the required reevaluation documentation are ineligible to participate in the DBE Program until they submit and are approved under the new eligibility standards. Submitting late is the only way to restore eligibility.

How long will a late reevaluation packet take to process?

Caltrans has not published a timeline for late packets—only that they are processed after the on-time queue. Every week of delay puts you further back in line, which is why assembling a complete packet quickly matters.

What does a late DBE reevaluation packet need to include?

The same items as an on-time packet: a Personal Narrative from each disadvantaged owner establishing individualized social and economic disadvantage, a current Personal Net Worth statement with complete supporting documentation, and updated ownership and control records. Incomplete packets risk denial or additional rounds of review.
