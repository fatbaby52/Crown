# Crown Consulting - Paid Search Strategy

## Overview

This document outlines a paid search (Google Ads) strategy to complement the SEO pages. The goal is to generate qualified leads for consultation bookings at a reasonable cost per acquisition.

## Budget Recommendation

**Starting budget:** $500-1,000/month
**Test period:** 8 weeks minimum before major adjustments
**Target CPA:** Under $500 (at $10k client value, this provides 20:1 return)

## Targeting Settings

**Geographic:** California only
**Language:** English

**Audience targeting (observation mode initially):**
- Small business owners
- Construction industry
- Government contractors

**Device:** All devices (mobile may perform well for these searches)

**Ad schedule:** Consider business hours only initially (Mon-Fri 7am-6pm PT) if budget is tight

## Bidding Strategy

**Start with:** Manual CPC or Maximize Clicks (with bid cap)
**Initial max CPC:** $10-15 for certification keywords, $15-20 for compliance keywords
**After 50+ conversions:** Consider Target CPA bidding

## Conversion Tracking

**Primary conversion:** Consultation form submission
**Secondary conversions:**
- Phone calls (if using call tracking)
- Click on booking link
- Time on site > 2 minutes

**Implementation needed:**
- Google Ads conversion tracking pixel on thank-you page
- Google Analytics 4 with conversion events
- Optional: Call tracking (CallRail, etc.)

## Landing Page Requirements

Each landing page should have:

1. **Headline** matching ad copy / search intent
2. **Clear value proposition** above the fold
3. **Consultation booking form** prominently placed (consider embedding Google Form or using Calendly)
4. **Phone number** visible
5. **Trust signals** (Tony and Alex bios, credentials)
6. **Mobile-optimized** design
7. **Fast load time** (< 3 seconds)

## Measurement & Optimization

### Week 1-2: Launch and learn
- Monitor impression share
- Check search terms report for irrelevant queries
- Add negative keywords as needed
- Verify conversion tracking is working

### Week 3-4: Initial optimization
- Pause underperforming keywords (high spend, no conversions)
- Adjust bids based on performance
- Test ad copy variations
- Review landing page bounce rates

### Week 5-8: Refine and scale
- Identify best-performing ad groups
- Shift budget toward winners
- Expand keywords for successful themes
- Consider increasing budget if CPA is acceptable

### Monthly ongoing
- Search terms review and negative keyword updates
- Ad copy refresh (prevent ad fatigue)
- Landing page A/B testing
- Budget reallocation based on performance

## Expected Performance (Estimates)

**Certification campaigns:**
- CPC: $5-12
- Conversion rate: 5-10%
- Cost per lead: $50-200

**Compliance campaigns:**
- CPC: $8-18
- Conversion rate: 3-7%
- Cost per lead: $100-400

These are estimates based on similar B2B service industries. Actual performance will vary.
